Questions

Read the hosting limits and billing details before choosing your slots.

Can I host any Discord bot?

OffsetTech currently hosts Modmail only. Custom code, third-party plugins and owner configuration commands are not supported. Private ticket-log viewing is not included in this version.

Can I host more than one bot?

Yes. Each slot hosts one bot with a separate database. Add slots as needed; availability is shown in your dashboard.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing in the dashboard and choose Manage subscriptions. Deleting or stopping a bot does not cancel its subscription.

Does signing in give you my Discord password?

No. Authentication happens through Discord. Your bot token is entered separately on the private deployment form, sent to the hosting platform and never displayed back in your browser.

BEFORE YOU START

A few things
to know.

Can I host any Discord bot?

This service currently hosts Modmail only. Custom code, third-party plugins and owner configuration commands are not supported. Private ticket-log viewing is not included in this version.

What does a slot include?

One hosted Modmail instance with its own database, plus dashboard and Discord lifecycle controls. You need a bot application owned by your Discord account.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing in your dashboard and choose Manage subscriptions. Deleting or stopping a bot does not cancel its subscription.

Do I need to share my Discord password?

No. Sign-in happens through Discord. Your bot token is entered separately on the private deployment form and sent to the hosting platform; it is never displayed back in your browser.
